Calories in 1 plate chicken biryani

What 1 plate chicken biryani comes to in calories, protein and carbohydrate, worked out from its ingredients.

Numbers worked out by Poshan Labs from IFCT 2017 (ICMR-NIN) and USDA FoodData Central. Last updated 16 October 2026.

One plate chicken biryani is about 498 kcal, with 22 g of protein, 53 g of carbohydrate, 3 g of fibre and 22 g of fat. Everything below is worked out from the ingredients, one by one.

Chicken biryani: calories from ½ to 2 plates

Each row is the whole dish at that amount, so you can read off what you ate.

What goes into it

This is a standard home recipe, not one of the sample-week dishes: these are the weights the numbers come from. Your cook's version will differ, so check the oil and the portion.

  • Oil: the groundnut oil is 88 kcal of the 498. Without the groundnut oil, 1 plate chicken biryani would come to about 409 kcal.
  • Measure it: the oil is the part of this number you decide, so measure it with a spoon rather than pouring.

Each ingredient links to its own page, and the thali calorie calculator has this dish, so you can add it to the rest of your plate.

How this was worked out

The page adds up the IFCT and USDA values for every ingredient at the weight of a standard home recipe. The weights are raw, as the tables measure them, and the oil is the oil in the recipe. Katori means a standard 150 ml bowl. Your cooking will differ a little, so treat the number as a good starting point and check it against how you feel and what the scale says.

Calories and nutrients are calculated from the Indian Food Composition Tables 2017, National Institute of Nutrition (ICMR), except curd, which uses USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy 171284 (plain whole-milk yogurt) because the Indian tables don't list it.
