Calories in 1 plate paneer tikka

What 1 plate paneer tikka comes to in calories, protein and carbohydrate, worked out from its ingredients.

Numbers worked out by Poshan Labs from IFCT 2017 (ICMR-NIN) and USDA FoodData Central. Last updated 16 October 2026.

One plate paneer tikka is about 208 kcal, with 13 g of protein, 11 g of carbohydrate, 1.1 g of fibre and 13 g of fat. Everything below is worked out from the ingredients, one by one.

There is a Jain version further down.

  • Oil: the groundnut oil is 27 kcal of the 208. Without the groundnut oil, 1 plate paneer tikka would come to about 181 kcal.
  • Measure it: the oil is the part of this number you decide, so measure it with a spoon rather than pouring.

How this was worked out

The page adds up the IFCT and USDA values for every ingredient at the weight used in the sample weeks. The weights are raw, as the tables measure them, and the oil is the oil in the recipe. Katori means a standard 150 ml bowl. Your cooking will differ a little, so treat the number as a good starting point and check it against how you feel and what the scale says.

Calories and nutrients are calculated from the Indian Food Composition Tables 2017, National Institute of Nutrition (ICMR), except curd, which uses USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy 171284 (plain whole-milk yogurt) because the Indian tables don't list it.
