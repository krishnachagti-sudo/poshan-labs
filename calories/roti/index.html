Calories in Indian dishes

Calories in 1 roti

What 1 roti comes to in calories, protein and carbohydrate, worked out from its ingredients.

Numbers worked out by Poshan Labs from IFCT 2017 (ICMR-NIN). Last updated 17 October 2026.

One roti is about 96 kcal, with 3.2 g of protein, 19 g of carbohydrate, 3.4 g of fibre and 0.5 g of fat. Everything below is worked out from the ingredients, one by one.

Roti: calories from 1 to 4 rotis

Each row is the whole dish at that amount, so you can read off what you ate.

AmountCalories (kcal)Protein (g)Carbs (g)Fat (g)
1 roti963.2190.5

4 rotis38413771.8

Small, medium or large roti

A roti is only as big as the atta in it. The sample charts count a medium one, 30 g of atta. A teaspoon (5 g) of ghee on top adds about 44 kcal.

How this was worked out

The page adds up the IFCT values for every ingredient at the weight used in the sample weeks. The weights are raw, as the tables measure them. Katori means a standard 150 ml bowl. Your cooking will differ a little, so treat the number as a good starting point and check it against how you feel and what the scale says.

Calories and nutrients are calculated from the Indian Food Composition Tables 2017, National Institute of Nutrition (ICMR).
