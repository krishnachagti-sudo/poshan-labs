Calories in 1 bowl ragi porridge with milk

What 1 bowl ragi porridge with milk comes to in calories, protein and carbohydrate, worked out from its ingredients.

Numbers worked out by Poshan Labs from IFCT 2017 (ICMR-NIN). Last updated 18 October 2026.

One bowl ragi porridge with milk is about 190 kcal, with 6.7 g of protein, 24 g of carbohydrate, 2.8 g of fibre and 7.2 g of fat. Everything below is worked out from the ingredients, one by one.

Ragi porridge with milk: calories from ½ to 2 bowls

How this was worked out

The page adds up the IFCT values for every ingredient at the weight used in the sample diet charts. The weights are raw, as the tables measure them. Katori means a standard 150 ml bowl. Your cooking will differ a little, so treat the number as a good starting point and check it against how you feel and what the scale says.

Calories and nutrients are calculated from the Indian Food Composition Tables 2017, National Institute of Nutrition (ICMR).
