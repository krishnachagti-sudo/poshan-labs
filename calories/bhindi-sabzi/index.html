Calories in Indian dishes

Calories in 1 katori bhindi sabzi

What 1 katori bhindi sabzi comes to in calories, protein and carbohydrate, worked out from its ingredients.

Numbers worked out by Poshan Labs from IFCT 2017 (ICMR-NIN). Last updated 18 October 2026.

One katori bhindi sabzi is about 81 kcal, with 2.4 g of protein, 5.5 g of carbohydrate, 4.6 g of fibre and 5.3 g of fat. Everything below is worked out from the ingredients, one by one.

There is a Jain version further down.

What goes into it

This is how 1 katori bhindi sabzi is built in the sample diet charts. Each ingredient links to its own page.

IngredientAmountCalories (kcal)

Onion (Pyaz)20 g10

Total81
  • Oil: the groundnut oil is 44 kcal of the 81. Without the groundnut oil, 1 katori bhindi sabzi would come to about 37 kcal.
  • Measure it: the oil is the part of this number you decide, so measure it with a spoon rather than pouring.

The Jain version

One katori bhindi sabzi (Jain) is about 75 kcal, with 2.2 g of protein, 4 g of carbohydrate, 4.3 g of fibre and 5.3 g of fat.

The Jain diet charts use a version that:

How this was worked out

The page adds up the IFCT values for every ingredient at the weight used in the sample diet charts. The weights are raw, as the tables measure them, and the oil is the oil in the recipe. Katori means a standard 150 ml bowl. Your cooking will differ a little, so treat the number as a good starting point and check it against how you feel and what the scale says.

Calories and nutrients are calculated from the Indian Food Composition Tables 2017, National Institute of Nutrition (ICMR).
