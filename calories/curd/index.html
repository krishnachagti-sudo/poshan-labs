Calories in 1 katori curd

What 1 katori curd comes to in calories, protein and carbohydrate, worked out from its ingredients.

Numbers worked out by Poshan Labs from IFCT 2017 (ICMR-NIN) and USDA FoodData Central. Last updated 15 October 2026.

One katori curd is about 61 kcal, with 3.5 g of protein, 4.7 g of carbohydrate and 3.2 g of fat. Everything below is worked out from the ingredients, one by one.

How this was worked out

The page adds up the IFCT and USDA values for every ingredient at the weight used in the sample weeks. The weights are raw, as the tables measure them. Katori means a standard 150 ml bowl. Your cooking will differ a little, so treat the number as a good starting point and check it against how you feel and what the scale says.

Calories and nutrients are calculated from the Indian Food Composition Tables 2017, National Institute of Nutrition (ICMR), except curd, which uses USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy 171284 (plain whole-milk yogurt) because the Indian tables don't list it.
