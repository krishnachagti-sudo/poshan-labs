Calories in 120 g chicken breast (raw weight), grilled

What 120 g chicken breast (raw weight), grilled comes to in calories, protein and carbohydrate, worked out from its ingredients.

Numbers worked out by Poshan Labs from IFCT 2017 (ICMR-NIN). Last updated 16 October 2026.

120 g chicken breast (raw weight), grilled is about 233 kcal, with 26 g of protein, 0.4 g of carbohydrate, 0.1 g of fibre and 14 g of fat. Everything below is worked out from the ingredients, one by one.

How this was worked out

The page adds up the IFCT values for every ingredient at the weight counted for it. The weights are raw, as the tables measure them, and the oil is the oil in the recipe. Katori means a standard 150 ml bowl. Your cooking will differ a little, so treat the number as a good starting point and check it against how you feel and what the scale says.

Calories and nutrients are calculated from the Indian Food Composition Tables 2017, National Institute of Nutrition (ICMR).
