Calories in Indian dishes

Calories in 1 katori toor dal

What 1 katori toor dal comes to in calories, protein and carbohydrate, worked out from its ingredients.

Numbers worked out by Poshan Labs from IFCT 2017 (ICMR-NIN). Last updated 18 October 2026.

One katori toor dal is about 143 kcal, with 7.2 g of protein, 19 g of carbohydrate, 3.9 g of fibre and 3.8 g of fat. Everything below is worked out from the ingredients, one by one.

There is a Jain version further down.

The Jain version

One katori toor dal (Jain, with hing) is about 136 kcal, with 6.9 g of protein, 18 g of carbohydrate, 3.6 g of fibre and 3.8 g of fat.

The Jain diet charts use a version that:

  • leaves out the onion (pyaz) and garlic (lehsan)
  • adds asafoetida (hing)

How this was worked out

The page adds up the IFCT values for every ingredient at the weight used in the sample diet charts. The weights are raw, as the tables measure them, and the oil is the oil in the recipe. Katori means a standard 150 ml bowl. Your cooking will differ a little, so treat the number as a good starting point and check it against how you feel and what the scale says.

Calories and nutrients are calculated from the Indian Food Composition Tables 2017, National Institute of Nutrition (ICMR).
