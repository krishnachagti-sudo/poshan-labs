Data

The food numbers come from the Indian Food Composition Tables 2017, published by the National Institute of Nutrition (ICMR), through the open IFCT 2017 compositions dataset (MIT licence).

Daily needs follow the ICMR-NIN Nutrient Requirements for Indians 2020.

Foods not in the Indian tables

A few foods people search for are not in the Indian tables. Their numbers come from USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy), published by the U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service.

It is in the public domain, and each page names the exact entry:

  • Oats: Cereals, oats, regular and quick, not fortified, dry (FDC 173904)
  • Sabudana: Tapioca, pearl, dry (FDC 169717)
  • Besan: Chickpea flour (besan) (FDC 174288)
  • Kabuli chana: Chickpeas (garbanzo beans, bengal gram), mature seeds, raw (FDC 173756)
  • Soya chunks: Soy flour, defatted (FDC 174275)
  • Chia seeds: Seeds, chia seeds, dried (FDC 170554)
  • Curd: Yogurt, plain, whole milk (FDC 171284)
