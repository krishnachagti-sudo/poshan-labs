      You asked.

      Q. 01Do I have to give up my favourite foods?

      No. Your plan starts from what you already eat. We change portions, timing and a few swaps, not your whole kitchen.

      Q. 02How soon will I see a difference?

      Every body responds differently, and weight usually moves slowly. We check in every week, so from the first week you'll see what's changing and we adjust the plan together.

      Q. 03Do I need to live near you?

      No. I work with clients across India and abroad, including New Zealand, over phone calls and WhatsApp.

      Q. 04Will I get support after my plan?

      Yes. Weekly check-ins and day-to-day guidance are part of every plan, and the plan changes as you do.
