Protein calculator for women: how much protein a day

Most Indian diets are short on protein, especially vegetarian ones. This tells you roughly how much you need in a day.

Written by Poshan Labs. Sources are named on the page. Last updated 16 October 2026.

Your answer will show here when the boxes are filled in correctly.

How much protein do I need a day?

ICMR-NIN (2020) recommends about 0.83 g of protein per kg of body weight a day for a healthy adult, and this calculator uses the same. It uses more in two cases:

  • 1.0 g per kg when you are losing weight, to help keep muscle
  • 1.2 g per kg if you train hard

With kidney disease, check with your doctor first.

How this works

For a healthy adult, ICMR-NIN (2020) recommends about 0.83 g of protein per kg of body weight a day, and that is what this calculator uses for staying healthy. When you're losing weight it uses 1.0 g per kg, which helps keep muscle while the fat comes off, and 1.2 g per kg if you train hard. Above 120 kg it counts 120 kg, because at a high weight much of it is not muscle. If you have kidney disease, check with your doctor before eating more protein.

A number is a starting point, not a plan. On a free call, Puja looks at it with your reports and your routine.
