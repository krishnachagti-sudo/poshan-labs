How can I estimate my body fat with a tape measure?

This uses the US Navy circumference method, which estimates body fat from:

  • your height
  • your neck and waist measurements
  • your hips as well, if you are a woman

It is usually within a few percentage points of a lab measurement. A DEXA scan is the accurate way if you need an exact figure.

How this works

This uses the US Navy circumference method, which estimates body fat from your height and your neck, waist and (for women) hip measurements, using logarithms of the differences. It's usually within a few percentage points of a lab measurement. A DEXA scan is the accurate way.

A number is a starting point, not a plan. On a free call, Puja looks at it with your reports and your routine.
