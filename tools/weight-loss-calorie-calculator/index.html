How many calories should I eat to lose weight?

Start from your maintenance calories, then eat:

  • 500 kcal a day below that for a steady loss of about half a kilo a week
  • or 300 kcal below for a gentler pace

Neither goes under 1,200 kcal for women or 1,500 kcal for men, because eating less without supervision makes it hard to get enough protein and nutrients.

How this works

First your maintenance calories are worked out with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation and your activity level. The steady target is 500 kcal a day below that, which usually means about half a kilo a week. The gentle target is 300 kcal below. Neither goes under 1,200 kcal for women or 1,500 kcal for men, because eating less than that without supervision makes it hard to get enough protein and nutrients.

A number is a starting point, not a plan. On a free call, Puja looks at it with your reports and your routine.
