What is a healthy weight for my height?

A healthy weight for an Indian adult is the weight that gives a BMI between 18.5 and 22.9, the WHO Asia-Pacific range.

  • How it is worked out: the calculator multiplies 18.5 and 22.9 by your height in metres, squared.
  • Why a range: frame, muscle and age all move where you will feel best within it.

How this works

It's the weight that gives a BMI between 18.5 and 22.9, the healthy range for Asian Indians (WHO Asia-Pacific). That's 18.5 and 22.9 multiplied by your height in metres, squared. It's a range on purpose: frame, muscle and age all move where you'll feel best within it.

A number is a starting point, not a plan. On a free call, Puja looks at it with your reports and your routine.
