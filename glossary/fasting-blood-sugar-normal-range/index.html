Fasting blood sugar normal range, and what your number means

A normal fasting blood sugar is below 100 mg/dL, which is about 5.6 mmol/L. From 100 to 125 mg/dL is the prediabetes range, and 126 mg/dL or more is the diabetes range, by the American Diabetes Association's cut-offs. Your number sits somewhere on this ladder.

Written by Poshan Labs. Sources are named on the page. Last updated 16 October 2026.

What your number means

One fasting reading is one morning. A bad night, stress or a late dinner can nudge it, so your doctor looks at it together with your HbA1c and your symptoms.

Your numberWhat it meansSource
85 mg/dLNormal. That is about 4.7 mmol/L.American Diabetes Association
99 mg/dLStill normal, the highest number in the normal band. That is about 5.5 mmol/L.CDC
100 mg/dLThe first number of the prediabetes range, about 5.6 mmol/L. It is worth showing your doctor and repeating.American Diabetes Association
110 mg/dLInside the prediabetes range, about 6.1 mmol/L. Your HbA1c tells your doctor whether this is a one-off or a pattern.American Diabetes Association
126 mg/dLThe diabetes cut-off, 7.0 mmol/L. A doctor usually confirms it with a repeat test before diagnosing.American Diabetes Association
150 mg/dLWell into the diabetes range, about 8.3 mmol/L. Please take the report to your doctor, and do not wait for your next scheduled test.American Diabetes Association

The ranges

Fasting means you have had nothing but water overnight before the blood is drawn. The same cut-offs are shown in both units, because Indian reports use both.

Things to keep in mind

  • Fasting sugar shows how your body is doing after a night without food. It does not show what happens after meals, which is what the glucose tolerance test (OGTT) and your HbA1c fill in.
  • If you have your fasting insulin too, the site's HOMA-IR calculator turns the two into one number that estimates insulin resistance. It is not a diagnosis, and doctors use different cut-offs.
  • A nutritionist never changes a medicine, a dose or its timing. That is for your doctor. What Puja does is look at your plate and your routine alongside your reports.
