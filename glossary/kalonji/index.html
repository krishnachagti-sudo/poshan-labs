What is kalonji (nigella seeds)?

Kalonji, also called nigella seeds or black seed, are tiny, matt black, teardrop-shaped seeds with a mildly bitter, onion-like taste. You'll find them on naan, in achaar, in Bengali panch phoron and in many sabzis.

Kalonji: tiny, matt black, teardrop-shaped seeds with a mildly bitter, onion-like taste.

Kalonji and your health

As a spice, kalonji adds flavour and a little crunch to everyday food.

Black seed oil and capsules:

  • They are promoted for everything from diabetes to skin and joint problems.
  • The evidence so far comes from small studies and isn't strong enough to support using them for any specific condition.

Supplements are a different matter from a sprinkle in your food:

  • they may increase bleeding in people on blood thinners
  • they can cause stomach upset or rashes
  • they aren't advised in pregnancy

How to have kalonji

Enjoy kalonji the way Indian kitchens always have, and these everyday cooking amounts are simply part of food:

  • sprinkled on parathas and naan
  • crackled in a tadka for aloo or kaddu
  • added to pickles

If you're thinking about black seed oil or capsules, talk to your doctor first, especially if you:

  • take medicine for diabetes or a blood thinner
  • have surgery coming up

Never use it in place of treatment your doctor has prescribed.
