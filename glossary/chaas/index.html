What is chaas (buttermilk)?

Chaas is Indian buttermilk: dahi whisked with plenty of water and usually seasoned with roasted jeera, salt, mint, ginger or curry leaves. It's thin, cool and lightly sour, and a summer staple across North and West India.

Chaas and your health

Because it's mostly water, chaas is a refreshing way to stay hydrated, and it carries a little of the protein and calcium from the dahi it's made from.

It's far lighter than:

  • lassi
  • cold drinks
  • packaged juices

So it's a useful swap if you're trying to cut sugar or calories. Many people also find a glass with lunch settling on a hot day.

How to have chaas

Make chaas at home from plain dahi and keep the salt light. Flavour it instead with:

  • jeera
  • pudina
  • ginger
  • a tadka of curry leaves

Have it with a meal or as an afternoon drink in place of soft drinks or sweetened juices.

Keep in mind:

  • Packaged masala chaas often has added salt, so check the label if you're watching your blood pressure.
  • Your fluid needs go up in hot weather or when you're active, so keep water handy too.
  • Pale yellow urine is a good sign you're drinking enough.
