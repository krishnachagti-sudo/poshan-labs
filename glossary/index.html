Words from your reports and your kitchen

142 words from lab reports, diet charts and the Indian kitchen, each in plain language. Start with your report below, or find a word from A to Z.

Read your report

Have a report in your hand? Start with the normal ranges, then find your test in its group.

  • HbA1c normal range A normal HbA1c is below 5.7%.
  • TSH normal range for women A typical TSH range for adults starts at about 0.4 mIU/L, with an upper limit of about 4.0 to 4.5 mIU/L depending on the lab, and women are read against the same range when not pregnant.
  • Fasting blood sugar normal range A normal fasting blood sugar is below 100 mg/dL, which is about 5.6 mmol/L.

  • Anti-TPO antibodiesProteins made by your own immune system that attack thyroid peroxidase, an enzyme your thyroid needs to make…

B

  • BloatingWhen your tummy feels full, tight and uncomfortable, and it may look swollen too
  • BMI (body mass index)A number worked out from your weight and height: your weight in kilograms divided by your height in metres…
  • BMR (basal metabolic rate)The minimum number of calories your body needs just to keep going at complete rest: to breathe, keep your…
  • Body fat percentageThe share of your total body weight that is fat, rather than muscle, bone, water and organs

C

  • CalciumA mineral your body uses to build bones and teeth, where almost all of it is stored
  • CalorieA unit that measures the energy in food and drink

  • CravingsA craving is a strong, specific urge for a particular food, very often something sweet, salty or fried, that…
  • CreatinineA normal waste product made when your muscles work and some muscle tissue breaks down
  • CRP (C-reactive protein)A protein your liver makes when there is inflammation in your body

D

  • DEXA scanA quick, low-dose X-ray that measures how much calcium and other minerals are packed into your bones
  • DHEA-SA hormone made mostly by your adrenal glands, two small glands that sit on top of your kidneys
  • Dietary fatThe fat in the food you eat, from oils, ghee and butter to nuts, seeds, milk, meat and fish

E

F

  • Fasting blood sugarThe amount of glucose in your blood after you have not eaten anything, usually overnight

  • Fatty liver (NAFLD)When too much fat builds up in your liver
  • Fermented foodsFoods in which friendly bacteria or yeast have broken down some of the sugars and starches
  • FerritinA protein that stores iron inside your body
  • FibreThe part of plant foods that your body can't fully digest
  • FolateVitamin B9
  • FSH (follicle stimulating hormone)A hormone made by the pituitary gland in your brain

G

  • Gestational diabetesDiabetes that appears for the first time during pregnancy
  • GlucoseThe main sugar in your blood and your body's main source of energy
  • GlutenA protein found naturally in wheat, barley and rye
  • Glycemic indexA score from 0 to 100 that shows how quickly a food containing carbohydrate raises your blood sugar
  • Glycemic loadA score that looks at both how quickly a food raises your blood sugar (its glycemic index) and how much…

H

  • HaemoglobinThe iron-rich protein inside your red blood cells that carries oxygen from your lungs to the rest of your body
  • Hashimoto's thyroiditisAn autoimmune condition where your immune system mistakenly attacks your thyroid gland
  • HbA1cA blood test that shows your average blood sugar over the past two to three months
  • HDL cholesterolOften called good cholesterol
  • HOMA-IRA number worked out from two fasting blood tests, your fasting glucose and your fasting insulin
  • Hot flushes (hot flashes)A sudden wave of heat over your face, neck and chest, often with sweating and red, flushed skin
  • HydrationHaving enough water in your body to keep it working properly
  • HyperthyroidismWhen your thyroid gland makes more hormone than your body needs
  • HypothyroidismWhen the thyroid gland in your neck doesn't make enough thyroid hormone

  • ICMR-NINThe National Institute of Nutrition in Hyderabad, part of the Indian Council of Medical Research
  • Ideal body weightAn estimate of the weight that suits your height
  • IdliA soft, steamed cake made from a fermented batter of rice and urad dal

  • Insoluble fibreThe type of fibre that doesn't dissolve in water

  • Intermittent fastingAn eating pattern that switches between set periods of eating and not eating
  • IronA mineral your body uses to make haemoglobin, the part of your red blood cells that carries oxygen around…
  • Iron deficiencyYour body doesn't have enough iron

K

  • KatoriThe small steel or ceramic bowl that sits on an Indian thali, used to serve dal, sabzi, dahi or kheer
  • KhichdiRice and dal cooked together into a soft, one-pot meal, usually with haldi, a little ghee and a jeera tadka
  • Kuttu (buckwheat)Buckwheat, and kuttu ka atta is the flour made from its seeds

  • LassiA thick drink made by churning dahi with water or milk
  • LDL cholesterolOften called bad cholesterol

  • Metabolic syndromeMetabolic syndrome isn't one disease

O

  • OestrogenA group of female sex hormones made mainly by the ovaries

  • Oral glucose tolerance test (OGTT)The oral glucose tolerance test checks how your body handles a large dose of sugar

  • OsteoporosisA condition where your bones lose density and strength and become fragile, so they break much more easily
  • OxalateA natural compound found in many plant foods, such as spinach, nuts, peanuts, wheat bran and rhubarb

  • Postprandial blood sugarYour blood glucose measured after a meal, usually about two hours after you start eating
  • PotassiumA mineral and electrolyte that your heart, muscles and nerves need to work properly
  • PrebioticsTypes of carbohydrate, mostly fibre, that your body can't digest
  • PrediabetesYour blood sugar is higher than normal but not yet high enough to be called diabetes
  • ProbioticsLive microorganisms, mostly friendly bacteria and some yeasts, that are meant to benefit your health when you…
  • ProgesteroneA hormone made mainly by the ovaries after an egg is released each month
  • ProlactinA hormone made by the pituitary gland, a pea-sized gland at the base of your brain
  • ProteinA nutrient your body uses to build and repair cells, from muscle and skin to hair
  • PulsesThe dried seeds of legume plants: the dals, beans and grams in your kitchen

R

  • RDA (recommended dietary allowance)Recommended dietary allowance
  • Refined carbohydratesGrains and sugars that have been processed until most of their fibre, vitamins and minerals are gone

S

  • SarcopeniaThe gradual loss of muscle size, strength and function that comes with age
  • SatietyThe feeling of being comfortably full and satisfied after eating, the signal that tells you to stop and keeps…

  • Saunf (fennel seeds)The seed of the fennel plant: small, greenish and slightly sweet, with an aniseed taste
  • SGPT (ALT)An enzyme found mostly in your liver
  • SHBGA protein made mostly by your liver
  • Singhara attaFlour made from dried singhara, the horned fruit of the water caltrop (Trapa), a plant that grows in ponds…
  • SodiumA mineral and one of your body's main electrolytes
  • Soluble fibreThe type of fibre that absorbs water and forms a soft gel in your gut during digestion

T

  • T-scoreThe main number on a bone density (DEXA) report
  • T3One of the two main hormones your thyroid gland makes
  • T4The main hormone made by your thyroid gland in the front of your neck
  • TDEE (total daily energy expenditure)The total number of calories your body burns in a whole day
  • TestosteroneA hormone that both men and women make, though women make much smaller amounts
  • Thermic effect of foodThe extra energy your body uses to digest, absorb and store the food you've just eaten
  • Total cholesterolThe total amount of cholesterol in your blood, including both LDL (bad) and HDL (good) cholesterol
  • Trans fatA harmful type of fat
  • TriglyceridesA type of fat in your blood
  • TriphalaA traditional Ayurvedic mix of three dried fruits: amla, haritaki and bibhitaki, usually sold as a powder or…
  • TSH (thyroid stimulating hormone)A hormone made by the pituitary gland in your brain
  • Type 2 diabetesA long-term condition where your blood sugar stays too high because your body doesn't make enough insulin or…

U

  • UpmaA savoury South Indian breakfast made by roasting suji (semolina) and cooking it with water, vegetables and a…
  • Uric acidA normal waste product your body makes when it breaks down purines, natural substances found in your own…

V

  • Visceral fatThe fat stored deep inside your belly, packed around organs such as your liver, heart, kidneys and intestines
  • Vitamin B12A vitamin your body needs to make healthy red blood cells and keep your nerves working
  • Vitamin CA vitamin your body uses to grow and repair tissues
  • Vitamin DA vitamin your skin makes when it is exposed to sunlight, and you get a little from food

Y
