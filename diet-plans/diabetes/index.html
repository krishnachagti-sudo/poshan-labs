4 sample days, each at three calorie levels, for diabetes: vegetarian, non-veg, eggetarian and Jain, with every number calculated.

Diabetes diet chart, Indian vegetarian: a sample day

The 1,500 calorie vegetarian sample day, meal by meal.

  1. 6:30 amEarly morning1 glass soaked methi seeds water, 10 soaked almonds85 kcal
  2. 8:30 amBreakfast1 besan chilla with paneer, 1½ katoris curd279 kcal
  3. 11:00 amMid-morning1½ oranges73 kcal
  4. 1:30 pmLunch2 jowar rotis, 1½ katoris moong dal, ½ katori cabbage and peas sabzi, 1 katori cucumber raita523 kcal
  5. 5:00 pmEvening1 katori moong sprouts chaat, 1 cup green tea, no sugar101 kcal
  6. 8:00 pmDinner1½ katoris masoor dal, ½ katori mixed vegetable sabzi, 2 rotis441 kcal

Day total: 1,502 kcal, 71 g protein.
