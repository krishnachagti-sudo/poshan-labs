Weight loss diet chart, Indian vegetarian: a sample day

Day 1 of the 1,500 calorie vegetarian week, meal by meal.

  1. 6:30 amEarly morning1 glass warm lemon water, 5 soaked almonds41 kcal
  2. 8:30 amBreakfast2 moong dal chillas, ½ katori curd286 kcal
  3. 11:00 amMid-morning2 katoris papaya72 kcal
  4. 1:30 pmLunch1 roti, 2 katoris masoor dal, 1½ katoris mixed vegetable sabzi, 1 plate salad (cucumber, tomato, onion)536 kcal
  5. 5:00 pmEvening1 small katori roasted chana, 1 cup green tea, no sugar86 kcal
  6. 8:00 pmDinner2 bowls tomato and vegetable soup, 2 besan chillas with paneer479 kcal

Day total: 1,500 kcal, 74 g protein.
