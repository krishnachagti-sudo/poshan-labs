How to use these

Each plan is a sample week: seven days of six or seven meals, with times, portions in katoris and rotis, and the calories and protein worked out.

  1. Pick your goal and the way you eat.
  2. Start at 1,500 calories, then move up or down.

They're here so you can see that eating well for PCOS, diabetes, menopause or weight loss doesn't mean giving up Indian food.

They're not personal plans. Yours is built from your reports and your routine, and it changes every week.
