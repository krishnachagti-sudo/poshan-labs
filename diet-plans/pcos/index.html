PCOS diet chart, Indian vegetarian: a sample day

The 1,500 calorie vegetarian sample day, meal by meal.

  1. 6:30 amEarly morning1 glass soaked methi seeds water, 10 soaked almonds85 kcal
  2. 8:30 amBreakfast2 besan chillas with paneer374 kcal
  3. 11:00 amMid-morning1½ guavas73 kcal
  4. 1:30 pmLunch1 ragi and wheat roti, 2 katoris rajma, 1½ katoris cucumber raita, 1 plate salad (cucumber, tomato, onion)529 kcal
  5. 5:00 pmEvening1 small katori roasted chana, 1 cup green tea, no sugar86 kcal
  6. 8:00 pmDinner1 katori dal palak, ½ katori lauki sabzi, 2 rotis350 kcal
