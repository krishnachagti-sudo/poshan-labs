PCOS diet chart: Indian Jain, 1,500 calories, 7 days

A sample week built around steady blood sugar: protein and fibre at every main meal, and carbohydrates that come with fibre. Use it to see what good days can look like, not as your personal plan.

Numbers worked out by Poshan Labs from IFCT 2017 (ICMR-NIN) and USDA FoodData Central. Last updated 16 October 2026.

Diet: Vegetarian Non-vegetarian Eggetarian Jain

Calories: 1,200 1,500 1,800

Not sure which level? Start at 1,500, or work yours out: daily calories or calories for weight loss.

Day 1, meal by meal

Dinner is at 6:30 pm because many Jain families finish eating before sunset (chauvihar).

  • From October to February the sun sets before 6:30 pm in Delhi, Kolkata and Chennai, so in those months move the evening snack and dinner earlier.
  • If your family eats after dark, the same plates work at your usual times.

Every day has at least 55 g of protein.

ICMR-NIN's 2020 allowance is 0.83 g per kg a day, about 54 g for its 65 kg reference man and 46 g for its 55 kg reference woman. The protein calculator gives your own number.

How the numbers were worked out

Calories and nutrients are calculated from the Indian Food Composition Tables 2017, National Institute of Nutrition (ICMR), except curd, which uses USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy 171284 (plain whole-milk yogurt) because the Indian tables don't list it. Fibre is IFCT's total dietary fibre, which counts more of a food's fibre than the crude fibre in older Indian tables, so a day reads higher than you may expect. Cooked portions assume home cooking with the oil listed in each dish. Katori means a standard 150 ml bowl.

Why the week looks like this

This week is built around steady blood sugar: protein and fibre at every main meal, and carbohydrates that come with fibre.

It keeps everyday Indian food at the centre, so there's nothing on it you'd struggle to cook or find.

This is a sample, not your plan

A sample week shows what eating for PCOS can look like. It can't know your reports, your medicines, your routine or what you can't stand eating.

Your own plan starts from all of those, and it changes every week as Puja sees how your body responds.

Medicines, pregnancy and breastfeeding

If you take medicine for diabetes or thyroid, or you're pregnant or breastfeeding, don't switch to a sample plan on your own. Talk to your doctor, or to Puja on a free call.
