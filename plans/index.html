How it works, in four steps

  1. 1Book your free consultation call

    We talk about your goals and I explain the plans and what you'll get, in detail. You can ask me anything.

  2. 2Your assessment

Questions

Do I get a complete personalised nutrition plan?

Yes. It's written from your assessment form and your reports, not picked from a template.

What health goals can you help with?

Weight loss, PCOS and PCOD, perimenopause and menopause, diabetes, thyroid, gut health, pregnancy and postpartum weight, weight gain, and general metabolic health.

Do I have to give up my favourite foods?

No. We keep what you love and change the portions, timing and pairings around it.

How soon can I expect results?

Every body responds differently, and weight usually moves slowly. We check in every week, so from the first week you'll see what's changing and we adjust the plan together.

How long should I sign up for?

We decide that together on your free call, based on your goal and your reports. Plans start from ₹3,500 a month.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. I work with clients across India and abroad, including New Zealand, over phone calls and WhatsApp. Pricing for clients outside India is a little different, so we'll talk about it on the call.
