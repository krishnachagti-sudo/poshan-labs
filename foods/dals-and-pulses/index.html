Dals and pulses, compared

The 21 dals and pulses on this site, with calories and protein for a serving of each, and calories, protein, carbohydrate and fibre per 100 g on wider screens. Per 100 g, among the foods from the Indian tables, the highest fibre is kala chana (25.2 g), the highest protein is soya bean (35.6 g) and the lowest calories are in whole toor (274 kcal).
