Safflower oil (blended): Nutrition and who it suits

Blended safflower oil is best kept small for weight loss, PCOS, diabetes and menopause. 100 g has 884 kcal. One serving is one teaspoon (5 g), about 44 kcal.

How many calories in one serving of blended safflower oil?

About 44 kcal for one teaspoon (5 g), and about 88 kcal if you have two.
