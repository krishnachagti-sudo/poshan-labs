Rajma (kidney beans): Nutrition and who it suits

Rajma is a good choice for weight loss, PCOS and diabetes, and fine in a portion for menopause. 100 g has 298 kcal, 19 g of protein and 17.7 g of fibre. One serving is one katori cooked (30 g dry), about 89 kcal.

Numbers worked out by Poshan Labs from IFCT 2017 (ICMR-NIN), using the rules on How the ratings work. Last updated 16 October 2026.

About 89 kcal for one katori cooked (30 g dry), and about 178 kcal if you have two.

Is rajma good for weight loss, PCOS, diabetes and menopause?

Weight loss

Good choice

6 g of fibre per 100 kcal keeps you full for longer.

PCOS and PCOD

Good choice

5.3 g of fibre for 14.9 g of carbohydrate, which slows the rise in sugar.

Diabetes

Good choice

5.3 g of fibre for 14.9 g of carbohydrate, which slows the rise in sugar.

Menopause

Fine as part of a meal

40 mg of calcium and 5.7 g of protein in a 30 g serving, not enough to call it a good source, but fine as part of a meal.
