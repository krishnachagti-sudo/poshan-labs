Foods / Mushrooms

Chicken mushroom, fresh: Nutrition and who it suits

Fresh chicken mushroom is a good choice for weight loss, PCOS and diabetes, and fine in a portion for menopause. 100 g has 21 kcal, 1.8 g of protein and 2 g of fibre. One serving is one katori (100 g), about 21 kcal.

How many calories in one serving of fresh chicken mushroom?

About 21 kcal for one katori (100 g), and about 42 kcal if you have two.
