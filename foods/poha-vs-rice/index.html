Poha vs rice: which is better?

Poha and rice side by side, goal by goal, with the numbers behind each rating.

Numbers worked out by Poshan Labs from IFCT 2017 (ICMR-NIN), using the rules on How the ratings work. Last updated 16 October 2026.

No winner on the ratings: poha and rice come out level for all four goals.

Where they differ, per 100 g, dry and before cooking:

  • Poha: 354 kcal, 7.4 g of protein and 3.5 g of fibre
  • Rice: 356 kcal, 7.9 g of protein and 2.8 g of fibre

Cooked, as they are counted in the sample diet charts:

  • 1 plate vegetable poha comes to about 222 kcal, with 5.1 g of protein and 35.7 g of carbohydrate.
  • 1 katori plain rice comes to about 143 kcal, with 3.2 g of protein and 31.3 g of carbohydrate.

Not quite like for like:

  • A plate and a katori are different amounts of food: one plate of vegetable poha starts from 40 g of poha and one katori of plain rice from 40 g of white rice.
  • Vegetable poha also has green peas, groundnut oil, mustard seeds and lemon, which plain rice does not.

Which is better, goal by goal

Weight loss They rate the same for weight loss.

They rate the same for weight loss. Per 100 g, dry and before cooking, poha has 354 kcal and 3.5 g of fibre; rice has 356 kcal and 2.8 g of fibre. Poha has more fibre. The rating goes by a normal serving, and at a serving the difference is not enough to put either ahead.

  • Poha, one plate of poha (40 g dry): eat in moderation. 141 kcal in a 40 g serving, without much fibre or protein to fill you up.
  • Rice, one katori cooked (40 g dry): eat in moderation. 143 kcal in a 40 g serving, without much fibre or protein to fill you up.
PCOS and diabetes They rate the same for PCOS and diabetes.

They rate the same for PCOS and diabetes. Per 100 g, dry and before cooking, poha has 76.8 g of carbohydrate and 3.5 g of fibre; rice has 78.2 g of carbohydrate and 2.8 g of fibre. Poha has more fibre. The rating goes by a normal serving, and at a serving the difference is not enough to put either ahead.

  • Poha, one plate of poha (40 g dry): limit. About 30.7 g of carbohydrate in a 40 g serving, with very little fibre to slow it.
  • Rice, one katori cooked (40 g dry): limit. About 31.3 g of carbohydrate in a 40 g serving, with very little fibre to slow it.
Menopause They rate the same for menopause.

They rate the same for menopause. Per 100 g, dry and before cooking, poha has 9.2 mg of calcium and 7.4 g of protein; rice has 7.5 mg of calcium and 7.9 g of protein. Poha has more calcium; rice has more protein. The rating goes by a normal serving, and at a serving the difference is not enough to put either ahead.

  • Poha, one plate of poha (40 g dry): fine as part of a meal. 3 g of protein in a 40 g serving, not enough to call it a good source, but fine as part of a meal.
  • Rice, one katori cooked (40 g dry): fine as part of a meal. 3.2 g of protein in a 40 g serving, not enough to call it a good source, but fine as part of a meal.

What is in each

What is in each, so you can compare the ingredients as well as the totals:

  • Vegetable poha, per plate: poha 40 g, green peas 20 g, onion 20 g, groundnut oil 5 g, mustard seeds 1 g, turmeric powder 1 g, lemon 5 g and curry leaves 1 g.
  • Plain rice, per katori: white rice 40 g.

The weights are raw, as the tables measure them, and the oil is the oil in the recipe. Your cooking will differ a little, so treat these as a starting point.

How this was decided

  • Same rule: each goal uses the same rule as every food page, so this page cannot disagree with them. How the ratings work shows the thresholds.
  • Serving: each food is rated at its own normal serving, as on its own page: one plate of poha (40 g dry) and one katori of cooked rice (40 g dry).
  • On its own: a rating is about the food on its own; the oil it is cooked in, what else is on the plate and your own reports change the real answer.
  • Too close: where two ratings differ only because the numbers that decide them sit within 10% of each other, either side of a threshold, the page calls it too close rather than name a winner.

Read each food on its own page: Poha and Rice. Ask Puja or your doctor before you swap one for the other if you take medicine for diabetes or thyroid.
