Groundnut oil: Nutrition and who it suits

Groundnut oil is best kept small for weight loss, PCOS, diabetes and menopause. 100 g has 884 kcal. One serving is one teaspoon (5 g), about 44 kcal.

How many calories in one serving of groundnut oil?

About 44 kcal for one teaspoon (5 g), and about 88 kcal if you have two.

How it compares

  • Saturated: the kind to keep small, especially for heart health and diabetes.
  • Mono: monounsaturated, the main fat in mustard and groundnut oil.
  • Poly: polyunsaturated, with the omega-3 and omega-6 fats your body can't make.
