Foods / Cereals and millets

Parboiled rice (Usna chawal): Nutrition and who it suits

Parboiled rice is fine in a portion for weight loss and menopause, and best kept small for PCOS and diabetes. 100 g has 352 kcal, 7.8 g of protein and 3.7 g of fibre. One serving is one katori cooked (40 g dry), about 141 kcal.

Numbers worked out by Poshan Labs from IFCT 2017 (ICMR-NIN), using the rules on How the ratings work. Last updated 17 October 2026.

How many calories in one serving of parboiled rice?

About 141 kcal for one katori cooked (40 g dry), and about 282 kcal if you have two.

Is parboiled rice good for weight loss, PCOS, diabetes and menopause?

Weight loss

Eat in moderation

141 kcal in a 40 g serving, without much fibre or protein to fill you up.

PCOS and PCOD

Limit

About 30.9 g of carbohydrate in a 40 g serving, with very little fibre to slow it.

Diabetes

Limit

About 30.9 g of carbohydrate in a 40 g serving, with very little fibre to slow it.

Menopause

Fine as part of a meal

3.1 g of protein in a 40 g serving, not enough to call it a good source, but fine as part of a meal.
