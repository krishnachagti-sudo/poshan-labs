Dates (khajoor): Nutrition and who it suits

Dates are fine in a portion for weight loss, and best kept small for PCOS, diabetes and menopause. 100 g has 320 kcal, 2.5 g of protein and 8.9 g of fibre. One serving is three dates (24 g), about 77 kcal.

Numbers worked out by Poshan Labs from IFCT 2017 (ICMR-NIN), using the rules on How the ratings work. Last updated 16 October 2026.
