Which is better, goal by goal

Weight loss They rate the same for weight loss.

They rate the same for weight loss. Per 100 g, dry and before cooking, jowar has 334 kcal and 10.2 g of fibre; bajra has 348 kcal and 11.5 g of fibre. Bajra has more fibre. The rating goes by a normal serving, and at a serving the difference is not enough to put either ahead.

  • Jowar, one roti's worth of flour, or a small katori cooked (30 g dry): good choice. 3.1 g of fibre per 100 kcal keeps you full for longer.
  • Bajra, one roti's worth of flour, or a small katori cooked (30 g dry): good choice. 3.3 g of fibre per 100 kcal keeps you full for longer.
PCOS and diabetes They rate the same for PCOS and diabetes.

They rate the same for PCOS and diabetes. Per 100 g, dry and before cooking, jowar has 67.7 g of carbohydrate and 10.2 g of fibre; bajra has 61.8 g of carbohydrate and 11.5 g of fibre. Jowar has more carbohydrate; bajra has more fibre. The rating goes by a normal serving, and at a serving the difference is not enough to put either ahead.

  • Jowar, one roti's worth of flour, or a small katori cooked (30 g dry): good choice. 3.1 g of fibre for 20.3 g of carbohydrate, which slows the rise in sugar.
  • Bajra, one roti's worth of flour, or a small katori cooked (30 g dry): good choice. 3.4 g of fibre for 18.5 g of carbohydrate, which slows the rise in sugar.
Menopause They rate the same for menopause.

They rate the same for menopause. Per 100 g, dry and before cooking, jowar has 27.6 mg of calcium and 10 g of protein; bajra has 27.4 mg of calcium and 11 g of protein. Bajra has more protein. The rating goes by a normal serving, and at a serving the difference is not enough to put either ahead.

  • Jowar, one roti's worth of flour, or a small katori cooked (30 g dry): fine as part of a meal. 3 g of protein in a 30 g serving, not enough to call it a good source, but fine as part of a meal.
  • Bajra, one roti's worth of flour, or a small katori cooked (30 g dry): fine as part of a meal. 3.3 g of protein in a 30 g serving, not enough to call it a good source, but fine as part of a meal.

How this was decided

  • Same rule: each goal uses the same rule as every food page, so this page cannot disagree with them. How the ratings work shows the thresholds.
  • Serving: each food is rated at its own normal serving, as on its own page: the same serving for both, one roti's worth of flour (30 g).
  • On its own: a rating is about the food on its own; the oil it is cooked in, what else is on the plate and your own reports change the real answer.
  • Too close: where two ratings differ only because the numbers that decide them sit within 10% of each other, either side of a threshold, the page calls it too close rather than name a winner.

Read each food on its own page: Jowar and Bajra. Ask Puja or your doctor before you swap one for the other if you take medicine for diabetes or thyroid.
