Duck, meat, with skin: Nutrition and who it suits

Duck meat with skin is a good choice for weight loss, PCOS, diabetes and menopause. 100 g has 131 kcal and 19.1 g of protein. One serving is one small portion (100 g), about 131 kcal.

How many calories in one serving of duck meat with skin?

About 131 kcal for one small portion (100 g), and about 262 kcal if you have two.
