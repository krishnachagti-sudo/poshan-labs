Which is better, goal by goal

Weight loss Egg is the better pick for weight loss.

Egg is the better pick for weight loss.

  • Egg, one egg (50 g): good choice. 9.1 g of protein per 100 kcal helps you stay full and keep muscle.
  • Paneer, a 50 g piece: eat in moderation. 129 kcal in a 50 g serving, with 9.4 g of protein, so watch the portion.
PCOS They rate the same for PCOS.

They rate the same for PCOS. Per 100 g, egg has almost no carbohydrate; paneer has 12.4 g of carbohydrate. Paneer has more carbohydrate. The rating goes by a normal serving, and at a serving the difference is not enough to put either ahead.

  • Egg, one egg (50 g): good choice. Almost no carbohydrate in a 50 g serving.
  • Paneer, a 50 g piece: good choice. 9.4 g of protein with only 6.2 g of carbohydrate in a 50 g serving, so it fills you without raising your sugar much.
Diabetes They rate the same for diabetes.

They rate the same for diabetes. Per 100 g, egg has almost no carbohydrate; paneer has 12.4 g of carbohydrate. Paneer has more carbohydrate. The rating goes by a normal serving, and at a serving the difference is not enough to put either ahead.

  • Egg, one egg (50 g): good choice. Almost no carbohydrate in a 50 g serving.
  • Paneer, a 50 g piece: good choice. Only 6.2 g of carbohydrate in a 50 g serving.
Menopause They rate the same for menopause.

They rate the same for menopause. Per 100 g, egg has 55.1 mg of calcium and 13.4 g of protein; paneer has 476 mg of calcium and 18.9 g of protein. Paneer has more calcium and protein. The rating goes by a normal serving, and at a serving the difference is not enough to put either ahead.

  • Egg, one egg (50 g): good choice. 6.7 g of protein in a 50 g serving helps you hold on to muscle.
  • Paneer, a 50 g piece: good choice. 238 mg of calcium in a 50 g serving, which your bones need more of now.

How this was decided

  • Same rule: each goal uses the same rule as every food page, so this page cannot disagree with them. How the ratings work shows the thresholds.
  • Serving: each food is rated at its own normal serving, as on its own page: one egg (50 g) and a 50 g piece of paneer.
  • On its own: a rating is about the food on its own; the oil it is cooked in, what else is on the plate and your own reports change the real answer.
  • Too close: where two ratings differ only because the numbers that decide them sit within 10% of each other, either side of a threshold, the page calls it too close rather than name a winner.
