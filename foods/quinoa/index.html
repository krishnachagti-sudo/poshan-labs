Quinoa: Nutrition and who it suits

Quinoa is a good choice for weight loss, PCOS and diabetes, and fine in a portion for menopause. 100 g has 328 kcal, 13.1 g of protein and 14.7 g of fibre. One serving is one katori cooked (40 g dry), about 131 kcal.

Numbers worked out by Poshan Labs from IFCT 2017 (ICMR-NIN), using the rules on How the ratings work. Last updated 17 October 2026.

How many calories in one serving of quinoa?

About 131 kcal for one katori cooked (40 g dry), and about 262 kcal if you have two.

Is quinoa good for weight loss, PCOS, diabetes and menopause?

Weight loss

Good choice

4.5 g of fibre per 100 kcal keeps you full for longer.

PCOS and PCOD

Good choice

5.9 g of fibre for 21.5 g of carbohydrate, which slows the rise in sugar.

Diabetes

Good choice

5.9 g of fibre for 21.5 g of carbohydrate, which slows the rise in sugar.

Menopause

Fine as part of a meal

79 mg of calcium and 5.2 g of protein in a 40 g serving, not enough to call it a good source, but fine as part of a meal.
