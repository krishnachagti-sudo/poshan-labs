Things to know before you use this list

  • Spoon foods: This list leaves out foods you eat by the pinch or the spoon, such as turmeric powder, cumin seeds, coriander seeds and halim seeds, because this list compares foods you eat by the katori or the piece, and per 100 g the spoon foods would top it. Turmeric powder has 46.1 mg of iron per 100 g, and a pinch to half a teaspoon (2 g) gives 0.9 mg. See its page.
  • Raw or dry: these are the values for the food as the tables measured it, raw or dry. Cooking changes them: dals and grains soak up water, and boiling vegetables moves some minerals into the water you throw away.
  • Iron from plants is taken up less easily than iron from meat, so the number on this list is not the whole story.
