Bhindi (okra): Nutrition and who it suits

Bhindi is a good choice for weight loss, PCOS and diabetes, and fine in a portion for menopause. 100 g has 27 kcal, 2.1 g of protein and 4.1 g of fibre. One serving is one katori (100 g), about 27 kcal.

Numbers worked out by Poshan Labs from IFCT 2017 (ICMR-NIN), using the rules on How the ratings work. Last updated 17 October 2026.

How many calories in one serving of bhindi?

About 27 kcal for one katori (100 g), and about 54 kcal if you have two.
