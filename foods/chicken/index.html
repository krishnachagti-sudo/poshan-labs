Chicken: Nutrition and who it suits

Chicken is a good choice for weight loss, PCOS, diabetes and menopause. 100 g has 168 kcal and 21.8 g of protein. One serving is one small portion (100 g), about 168 kcal.

Numbers worked out by Poshan Labs from IFCT 2017 (ICMR-NIN), using the rules on How the ratings work. Last updated 18 October 2026.

How many calories in one serving of chicken?

About 168 kcal for one small portion (100 g), and about 336 kcal if you have two.
