Things to know before you use this list

  • Spoon foods: This list leaves out foods you eat by the pinch or the spoon, such as fenugreek seeds, coriander seeds, cloves and chia seeds, because this list compares foods you eat by the katori or the piece, and per 100 g the spoon foods would top it. Fenugreek seeds have 47.5 g of fibre per 100 g, and a pinch to half a teaspoon (2 g) gives 1 g. See its page.
  • Raw or dry: these are the values for the food as the tables measured it, raw or dry. Cooking changes them: dals and grains soak up water, and boiling vegetables moves some minerals into the water you throw away.
  • The tables give total fibre. How much of it is soluble and how much is not is in the two glossary pages above.
