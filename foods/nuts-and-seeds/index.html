The 16 nuts and seeds on this site, with calories and protein for a serving of each, and calories, protein, fat and fibre per 100 g on wider screens. Per 100 g, among the foods from the Indian tables, the highest fibre is flax seeds (26.2 g), the highest protein is peanuts (23.6 g) and the lowest calories are in fresh coconut (409 kcal).
