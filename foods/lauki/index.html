Lauki (bottle gourd): Nutrition and who it suits

Lauki is a good choice for weight loss, PCOS and diabetes, and fine in a portion for menopause. 100 g has 11 kcal, 0.5 g of protein and 2.1 g of fibre. One serving is one katori (100 g), about 11 kcal.

Numbers worked out by Poshan Labs from IFCT 2017 (ICMR-NIN), using the rules on How the ratings work. Last updated 18 October 2026.

How many calories in one serving of lauki?

About 11 kcal for one katori (100 g), and about 22 kcal if you have two.
