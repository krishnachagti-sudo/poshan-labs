Corn oil: Nutrition and who it suits

Corn oil is best kept small for weight loss, PCOS, diabetes and menopause. 100 g has 884 kcal. One serving is one teaspoon (5 g), about 44 kcal.

How many calories in one serving of corn oil?

About 44 kcal for one teaspoon (5 g), and about 88 kcal if you have two.
