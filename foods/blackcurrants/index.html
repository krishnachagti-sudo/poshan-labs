Blackcurrants: Nutrition and who it suits

Blackcurrants are a good choice for weight loss, and fine in a portion for PCOS, diabetes and menopause. 100 g has 54 kcal, 1.5 g of protein and 4.1 g of fibre. One serving is one katori (100 g), about 54 kcal.

Numbers worked out by Poshan Labs from IFCT 2017 (ICMR-NIN), using the rules on How the ratings work. Last updated 17 October 2026.

How many calories in one serving of blackcurrants?

About 54 kcal for one katori (100 g), and about 108 kcal if you have two.

Are blackcurrants good for weight loss, PCOS, diabetes and menopause?

Weight loss

Good choice

Only 54 kcal per 100 g, so you can eat a full portion.

PCOS and PCOD

Eat in moderation

About 9.9 g of carbohydrate in a 100 g serving, 6.3 g of it sugar, so keep to this portion.

Diabetes

Eat in moderation

About 9.9 g of carbohydrate in a 100 g serving, 6.3 g of it sugar, so keep to this portion.

Menopause

Fine as part of a meal

40 mg of calcium in a 100 g serving, not enough to call it a good source, but fine as part of a meal.
