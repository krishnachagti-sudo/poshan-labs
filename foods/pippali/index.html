Pippali (Pipli): Nutrition and who it suits

Pippali is fine in small amounts for weight loss, PCOS, diabetes and menopause. 100 g has 217 kcal, 10.5 g of protein and 34.1 g of fibre. One serving is a pinch to half a teaspoon (2 g), about 4 kcal.

How many calories in one serving of pippali?

About 4 kcal for a pinch to half a teaspoon (2 g), and about 8 kcal if you have two.

One serving, against your day

One serving (2 g) fills less than 5% of an adult woman's daily need for every nutrient measured here. The largest share is potassium, at 1.1% (37 mg).
