Garlic (Lehsan): Nutrition and who it suits

Garlic is fine in small amounts for weight loss, PCOS, diabetes and menopause. 100 g has 124 kcal, 6.9 g of protein and 5.2 g of fibre. One serving is two cloves (6 g), about 7 kcal.

Numbers worked out by Poshan Labs from IFCT 2017 (ICMR-NIN), using the rules on How the ratings work. Last updated 18 October 2026.

How many calories in one serving of garlic?

About 7 kcal for two cloves (6 g), and about 14 kcal if you have two.

One serving, against your day

One serving (6 g) fills less than 5% of an adult woman's daily need for every nutrient measured here. The largest share is folate, at 2.3% (5.1 µg).

Source

Source: Indian Food Composition Tables 2017, National Institute of Nutrition (ICMR), food code G011. Daily need is from ICMR-NIN Nutrient Requirements for Indians 2020, adult woman, moderate activity.
