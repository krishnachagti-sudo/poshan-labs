Foods / Nuts and seeds

Safflower seeds (Kardi): Nutrition and who it suits

Safflower seeds are a good choice for diabetes, and fine in a portion for weight loss, PCOS and menopause. 100 g has 473 kcal, 17.7 g of protein and 13.5 g of fibre. One serving is a small handful (15 g), about 71 kcal.
