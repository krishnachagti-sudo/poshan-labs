Calcium rich foods without milk

If you cannot take milk, or simply do not like it, these are the foods that carry the most calcium, with nothing from milk, curd or paneer.

Numbers worked out by Poshan Labs from IFCT 2017 (ICMR-NIN) and USDA FoodData Central. Last updated 17 October 2026.

Ranked by what one normal serving gives, because a serving is what you eat, not 100 g.

  2. amaranth leaves (330 mg in one katori cooked)
  3. drumstick leaves (314 mg in one katori cooked)
  4. fenugreek leaves (274 mg in one katori cooked)

Things to know before you use this list

  • Spoon foods: This list leaves out foods you eat by the pinch or the spoon, such as gingelly seeds, poppy seeds, ajwain and cumin seeds, because this list compares foods you eat by the katori or the piece, and per 100 g the spoon foods would top it. Gingelly seeds have 1664 mg of calcium per 100 g, and one tablespoon (10 g) gives 166 mg. See its page.
  • Raw or dry: these are the values for the food as the tables measured it, raw or dry. Cooking changes them: dals and grains soak up water, and boiling vegetables moves some minerals into the water you throw away.
  • Milk, curd, paneer and khoa are left out on purpose.
