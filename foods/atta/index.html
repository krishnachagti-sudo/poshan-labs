Atta (whole wheat flour): Nutrition and who it suits

Atta is a good choice for weight loss, PCOS and diabetes, and fine in a portion for menopause. 100 g has 320 kcal, 10.6 g of protein and 11.4 g of fibre. One serving is one roti's worth of flour (30 g dry), about 96 kcal.

Numbers worked out by Poshan Labs from IFCT 2017 (ICMR-NIN), using the rules on How the ratings work. Last updated 16 October 2026.

How many calories in one serving of atta?

About 96 kcal for one roti's worth of flour (30 g dry), and about 192 kcal if you have two.
