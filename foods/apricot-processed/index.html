Apricot, processed (Khoomani): Nutrition and who it suits

Processed apricot is a good choice for weight loss, and fine in a portion for PCOS, diabetes and menopause. 100 g has 56 kcal, 1.5 g of protein and 0.6 g of fibre. One serving is one katori (100 g), about 56 kcal.

Numbers worked out by Poshan Labs from IFCT 2017 (ICMR-NIN), using the rules on How the ratings work. Last updated 16 October 2026.
