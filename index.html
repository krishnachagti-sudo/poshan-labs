What I can help with

Weight loss

You've cut sugar, skipped dinner, tried the soup diet and the 5 am walks. The weight came back anyway. I build plans that you can actually keep, out of the food you already cook.

Read more ›

PCOS and PCOD

Irregular periods, cravings, weight that collects around the middle and won't shift. Food won't do everything for PCOS, but the right food helps a lot, and it should still look like your own food.

Read more ›

Perimenopause and menopause

You're eating the way you always did, and suddenly the weight sits around your middle, sleep is worse, and your energy is gone. Your body has changed. Your food needs to change with it.

Read more ›

Diabetes

Living with diabetes or prediabetes shouldn't mean living with a list of foods you're scared of. I'll help you understand what raises your sugar, what steadies it, and how to eat normal Indian meals with your family.

Read more ›

What happens after you book

  1. We talk on the phone first.It's free, and you can ask me anything, including whether this is right for you at all.
  2. If you decide to go ahead, I'll ask for your recent health reports and have you fill a detailed assessment form about your routine: what you eat on a normal day, what you refuse to give up, when you get hungry, how you sleep.
  3. Then you choose a plan, and I write yours.From there we check in every week, I keep an eye on how you're doing day to day, and I change the plan when it stops working, when you travel, or when you're simply bored of it.

Questions people ask on the first call

Do I have to give up my favourite foods?

No. Your plan starts from what you already eat. We change portions, timing and a few swaps, not your whole kitchen.

How soon will I see a difference?

Every body responds differently, and weight usually moves slowly. We check in every week, so from the first week you'll see what's changing and we adjust the plan together.

Do I need to live near you?

No. I work with clients across India and abroad, including New Zealand, over phone calls and WhatsApp.

Will I get support after I receive my plan?

Yes. Weekly check-ins and day-to-day guidance are part of every plan, and the plan changes as you do.
