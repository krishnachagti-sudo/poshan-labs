Diabetes diet chart, Indian vegetarian: a sample day

The 1,500 calorie vegetarian sample day, meal by meal.

  1. 6:30 amEarly morning1 glass soaked methi seeds water, 10 soaked almonds85 kcal
  2. 8:30 amBreakfast1 besan chilla with paneer, 1½ katoris curd279 kcal
  3. 11:00 amMid-morning1½ oranges73 kcal
  4. 1:30 pmLunch2 jowar rotis, 1½ katoris moong dal, ½ katori cabbage and peas sabzi, 1 katori cucumber raita523 kcal
  5. 5:00 pmEvening1 katori moong sprouts chaat, 1 cup green tea, no sugar101 kcal
  6. 8:00 pmDinner1½ katoris masoor dal, ½ katori mixed vegetable sabzi, 2 rotis441 kcal

Day total: 1,502 kcal, 71 g protein.

Numbers worked out by Poshan Labs from IFCT 2017 (ICMR-NIN) and USDA FoodData Central (curd).

Who comes to me with this

People come to me with type 2 diabetes, prediabetes, insulin resistance or a worrying HbA1c, often after being given a generic diet chart. Many have stopped enjoying food because they're never sure what's allowed.

What I do differently

I look at your reports and your routine, then build meals that keep your blood sugar steadier:

  • the right portion of carbohydrate
  • more fibre and protein alongside it
  • timings that fit your day

Roti and rice don't disappear. We work out how much, with what, and when.

The plan always works alongside your doctor's treatment, never instead of it.

One of my clients went from 81.7 kg to 73.8 kg. In his words: "My insulin resistance, energy levels, and glycemic control have improved along the way."
